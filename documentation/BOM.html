2. Technical Component Roles & Procurement Checkpoints

Component Critical Technical Requirement Verification Method Prior to Assembly
Luckfox Pico Mini B Must feature 128MB SPI NAND onboard flash. Confirm board boots from internal flash via USB MaskRom without MicroSD card present.
SC3336 3MP Module Wide-angle optical lens (98.3 Deg FOV) with 15-pin FPC. Verify CSI ribbon cable pitch (0.5mm) and pin 1 GND orientation with board socket.
USB Wi-Fi Dongle Realtek RTL8188EUS chipset with SoftAP mode. Verify device ID 0bda:8179 in Linux kernel; confirm '8188eu' driver binds automatically.
LiPo Battery Pack 3.7V 1000mAh with internal PCM protection board. Verify open-circuit voltage (3.7V - 4.2V) and confirm JST-PH 2.0mm connector polarity (+/-).
IP5306 PMIC Module Load-sharing capable synchronous boost converter. Verify 5.0V output under 500mA load while simultaneously charging LiPo cell.
LM2596 Buck Regulator Step-down automotive pre-regulator (3A peak). Adjust trim potentiometer to precisely 5.00V DC before connecting downstream loads.
TVS Diode & Filter Network SMBJ5.0A (600W) + 470uF bulk reservoir capacitors. Verify TVS diode polarity (cathode towards +12V rail) and capacitor voltage ratings (25V min).
