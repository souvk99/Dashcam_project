4. Vehicle In-Line Charging & Thermal Dissipation Analysis

• Charging Dynamics: LM2596 delivers up to 2.0A at 5.0V. System draws 532mA; remaining ~1.0A charges LiPo to 100% in ~65 min.

• Thermal Dissipation: RV1103 SoC dissipates ~0.8W. Requires a 9x9x5mm extruded aluminum heatsink.

• Automotive Environment: Windshield temperatures can exceed 65 Deg C. LiPo charging must be inhibited above 45 Deg C ambient.
