2. Optical & Electrical Operating Parameters

Parameter Specification Engineering Note
Optical Format 1/2.8-inch Progressive Scan CMOS High-sensitivity pixel architecture for automotive night vision.
Active Resolution 2304 (H) x 1296 (V) pixels (3.0 Megapixels) Captures 1080p Full HD with zero scaling loss and 2K capability.
Pixel Size 2.0 um x 2.0 um BSI Technology High signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) with low dark-current noise.
Output Interface MIPI CSI-2 (2-lane, D-PHY specification) Differential signaling up to 800 Mbps per data lane.
Supply Rails AVDD: 2.8V, DOVDD: 1.8V, DVDD: 1.2V Filtered via low-noise LDOs and ferrite bead decoupling.

12 CAM_PDN Digital Input 3.3V / 1.8V Sensor power-down control line (pulled low for active state).
13 I2C_SCL Bidirectional 3.3V Open-Drain SCCB register programming clock with 2.2k pull-up to 3.3V.
14 I2C_SDA Bidirectional 3.3V Open-Drain SCCB register programming data with 2.2k pull-up to 3.3V.
15 VCC_3V3 Power Feed 3.3V DC (+/- 5%) Main power feed to onboard 2.8V AVDD low-noise LDO regulator.

4. Signal Integrity & Layout Constraints

• Differential impedance for MIPI data and clock pairs: 100 Ohm (+/- 10%) differential.

• Intra-pair length matching: skew less than 0.5mm between positive and negative traces.

• Dedicated solid ground reference plane directly beneath all MIPI differential routing.
