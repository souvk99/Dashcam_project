Carrier Board Specification: Hardware Architecture & Net Schedule

1. Carrier Board Design Objective & Functional Subsystems

The Dashcam Carrier Board (ENG-DASH-PCB-001) serves as the central electro-mechanical backplane integrating the Luckfox Pico Mini B SBC (RV1103), SmartSens SC3336 3MP Camera, 1000mAh LiPo Battery, IP5306 BMS Module, and automotive power conditioning.

4. High-Speed PCB Layout Guidelines & Physical Constraints

• MIPI CSI-2 Differential Pairs: Route MIPI_D0, MIPI_CLK, and MIPI_D1 as 100 Ohm differential controlled impedance microstrips over an unbroken solid ground plane (Layer 2). Keep intra-pair length skew below 0.5 mm (20 mils) and inter-pair skew below 1.5 mm.

• USB 2.0 Differential Routing: Route USB_DM and USB_DP as 90 Ohm differential pairs. Avoid via transitions between SBC and USB-A receptacle.

• Power Distribution: Form a wide polygon pour for +5V_SYS and +12V_IN rails (>35 mil trace width or plane pour) to minimize IR drop during Wi-Fi transmission bursts (up to 805 mA peak system draw).

• Physical Dimensions & Mounting: Form factor: 60.0 mm x 38.0 mm, 2-layer FR4, 1.6 mm thickness, 1 oz copper. Features 4x M2.5 mounting holes on a 53.0 mm x 31.0 mm rectangle matching the enclosure standoffs.

• Manufacturing Gerbers & 3D Renders: Production Gerbers (F_Cu, B_Cu, F_SilkS, B_SilkS, F_Mask, B_Mask, Edge_Cuts, Drill) exported to hardware/source/carrier_board/gerbers/. High-resolution raytraced 3D renders available in hardware/source/carrier_board/carrier_board_3d_top.png and carrier_board_3d_iso.png.
